## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Your Run

If you've got 45 minutes and want a satisfying session, these habits consistently pay off in player experience:

1. **Treat every room as a checklist.** Examine containers, shelves, and odd props before moving on. The demo hides items in plain sight.
2. **Combine aggressively.** Once you have two plausible items, try them together. The demo's crafting logic is forgiving but not signposted.
3. **Read the strange book.** The demo features a book with missing passages — it's a deliberate hook, not filler.
4. **Don't hoard healing items.** The demo is short enough that unused consumables are wasted consumables.
5. **Fight the enemy once.** You can avoid it entirely, but trading blows once teaches you how combat feels in the full game.
6. **Play with headphones.** The contrast between pixel art and live-action footage lands harder with proper audio.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to beat the Tenebris Somnia demo?

Most players report finishing in roughly 35 to 50 minutes on a normal first run. Speedrunners and walkthrough users can finish in about 20 to 30 minutes, while completionists who read every piece of environmental text may push past an hour. The Tenebris Somnia demo length is consistent because the slice is linear with no branching paths.

### Is the demo longer than a typical Steam Next Fest demo?

It sits comfortably in the middle-to-longer range. Many Next Fest demos are 15-to-20-minute vertical slices, so a 30-to-50-minute demo with multiple puzzles and cutscenes gives you noticeably more to chew on. It's still a taste, not a chapter.

### Can I replay the demo to get a different ending?

No. The demo follows a single linear path with no branching outcomes, so a second run is mostly about catching flavor text and details you missed the first time. That said, a second pass typically takes far less time — often 20 minutes or less.

### Does demo progress carry over to the full game?

There's no indication that demo saves transfer to the full release. Treat your demo run as a standalone experience and expect to start fresh when the complete game launches. If you're tracking the Tenebris Somnia demo length for planning purposes, assume your first full-game session will be its own thing entirely.
