A free demo is already downloadable through that same page, which is genuinely useful this close to release. You can verify performance on your own machine before spending anything — a real advantage when a game leans on live-action cutscenes that stream from disk.

### Why a PC-Only Launch Makes Sense

Tenebris Somnia blends two very different technical pipelines: a 2D pixel-art adventure layer and full-motion video sequences shot with real actors and practical effects by an award-winning film crew from Argentina. FMV-heavy titles carry large video payloads, and the store page confirms a 13 GB install. Porting that to consoles means re-encoding and re-certifying every cutscene for each platform holder — a meaningful cost for an independent studio.

For players, that means the practical answer to "what are the Tenebris Somnia platforms" is likely to stay "PC" for the foreseeable future. Treat any console listing you see without a New Blood Interactive announcement as unverified.

## PC System Requirements for Tenebris Somnia

The requirements are modest by 2026 standards, but the video sequences push the storage and VRAM numbers higher than the pixel art might suggest.

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Operating System | Windows 10 64-bit or later | Windows 11 64-bit |
| Processor | 2.4 GHz dual-core or higher | 3.6 GHz quad-core or higher |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | 16 GB RAM |
| Graphics | 4 GB VRAM or greater | 8 GB VRAM or greater |
| DirectX | Version 11 | Version 11 |
| Storage | 13 GB available space | 13 GB available space |

A few practical notes drawn from those figures:

- **8 GB of RAM is the floor, not the target.** Modern Windows alone eats a significant share of that. If you have 16 GB, you are comfortable.
- **VRAM matters more than raw GPU power here.** The 4 GB minimum reflects video decoding, not 3D rendering. Integrated graphics with shared memory often fall short.
- **13 GB is a fixed cost on both tiers.** Budget the space before launch night rather than during it.
- **DirectX 11 is the ceiling and the floor.** There is no DX12 requirement, which keeps older cards in play.
