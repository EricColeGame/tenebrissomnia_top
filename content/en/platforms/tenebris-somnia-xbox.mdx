### What Play Anywhere Does Not Cover

It is worth being precise: Play Anywhere is tied to digital purchases and the Microsoft ecosystem. Physical discs, where they exist for a given title, do not always unlock the PC copy, and progress syncing depends on being signed in with the same Microsoft account. If you plan to play across two devices, sign in consistently from day one.

### Why Single Player Matters for Horror

Horror games live or die on atmosphere, and atmosphere is hard to protect in a shared online space. A single-player structure means the pacing, scares, and puzzle difficulty are tuned to one person in one room — which is exactly the design philosophy behind live-action cutscenes intercut with 2D exploration.
