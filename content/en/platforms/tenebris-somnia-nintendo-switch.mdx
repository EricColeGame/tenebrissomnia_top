## What Kind of Horror Game Should You Expect?

Survival horror is a broad label, so it helps to break down what the listed gameplay tags usually translate to in practice.

- **Puzzle-driven progression** — keys, codes, environmental logic, and backtracking through spaces that change meaning once you understand them.
- **Combat with limits** — weapons that feel scarce, ammo that feels scarcer, and encounters you are sometimes meant to avoid rather than win.
- **Atmosphere over spectacle** — lighting, sound design, and pacing doing the heavy lifting.
- **Exploration as storytelling** — notes, rooms, and environmental detail replacing exposition dumps.

| Gameplay Pillar | What It Usually Means | What to Watch For |
|---|---|---|
| Puzzle design | Logic gates and item use | Whether puzzles respect your time |
| Combat | Deliberate, weighty encounters | Enemy variety and difficulty balance |
| Exploration | Interconnected, readable spaces | Map quality and backtracking fatigue |
| Atmosphere | Sound, lighting, art direction | Performance stability in dark scenes |

If Tenebris Somnia leans hard into the puzzle side, it will sit closer to classic fixed-camera horror than to modern action-horror. That distinction matters when you decide whether the $49.99 price tag feels right for you.
