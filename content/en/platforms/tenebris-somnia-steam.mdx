A few practical notes for planning your install:

- **Storage is identical for both tiers.** You need 13 GB free either way, so don't expect the recommended spec to demand more drive space.
- **VRAM is the real gate.** Going from 4 GB to 8 GB of video memory is a big jump, and it's the component most likely to separate comfortable play from compromises.
- **RAM doubles on the recommended tier.** 16 GB is now the practical baseline for modern releases, so most gaming rigs from the last few years clear this easily.
- **DirectX 11 is the floor for both tiers**, which keeps compatibility wide rather than locking the game to newer APIs.
- **If your PC sits between the two tiers**, target the minimum settings first and adjust from there once you can see real performance.

The good news: a 2D retro-styled game with live-action video segments is generally far less demanding than a full 3D horror title, so the minimum tier is a reasonable target for older hardware.
