## Gameplay, Visuals, and Live-Action Cutscenes

Tenebris Somnia wears its influences openly. The developers describe it as a survival horror adventure in the vein of Silent Hill and Resident Evil, rebuilt around a retro 2D aesthetic drawn from the 8-bit and 16-bit eras. You solve puzzles, manage scarce resources, and fight creatures that the store description bluntly calls horrible.

The twist is the presentation. At key story beats, the game cuts away to fully live-action sequences shot with real actors and practical effects by a professional, award-winning film crew from Argentina. That's a genuinely unusual production choice for a 2D indie horror game, and it's the element most likely to define the experience — the tagline teases it directly: "What if they aren't just nightmares?"
