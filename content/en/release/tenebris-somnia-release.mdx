## What Is Tenebris Somnia?

Tenebris Somnia is a survival horror adventure built in the spirit of genre classics like *Silent Hill* and *Resident Evil*, but reimagined through a retro 2D lens that evokes the 8- and 16-bit era. You solve puzzles, manage limited resources, and fight genuinely unsettling creatures while a disturbing story unfolds around you.

What separates it from the crowded indie horror field is its cinematics. At key story beats, the perspective shifts entirely into live-action sequences featuring real actors and practical effects, produced by a professional and award-winning film crew from Argentina. The pitch line says it best: *What if they aren't just nightmares?*
