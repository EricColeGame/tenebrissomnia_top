## What Is Tenebris Somnia?

Tenebris Somnia is a 2D retro survival-horror adventure game with live-action cutscenes. Rather than chasing photorealism, it leans into a deliberately old-school presentation: pixel-art exploration, puzzle solving, and tense creature encounters, punctuated by filmed sequences starring real actors and practical effects. One minute it looks like a lost 1990s horror cartridge; the next it looks like a low-budget Argentinian horror film — and that contrast is the point.

The project is led by creator Andrés Borghi and developed by Saibot Studios, with New Blood Interactive publishing. That publisher's catalog — ULTRAKILL, DUSK, and FAITH — tells you plenty about the tone: strange, fast, and proudly outside the AAA machine. Airdorf, the developer behind FAITH, executive produces Tenebris Somnia, and the game is widely framed as a spiritual successor to that cult classic.

The live-action sequences are a genuine selling point rather than a gimmick. Key cinematic moments shift to a completely different point of view, shot by a professional, award-winning film crew from Argentina. That mixed-media approach is also why the physical release has drawn collector interest: this is exactly the kind of odd, ambitious project that vanishes from shelves and reappears at triple the price.
