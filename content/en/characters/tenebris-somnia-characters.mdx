## Julia and Iván: The Two Pillars of the Story

### Julia — played by Clara Kovacic

Julia is the player's anchor. She's the one who dreams the monsters into existence, the one who chooses to walk into a corrupted apartment instead of calling the police, and the one whose emotional history with Iván gives the horror its teeth. Clara Kovacic carries the lead role, and everything the player experiences is filtered through her perspective.

That's a meaningful design choice. In a story where reality is unreliable, the protagonist's subjective experience becomes the only map you have. If Julia can't trust what she sees, neither can you.

### Iván — played by Pedro Merlo

Iván is the absent center of the entire plot — the ex-boyfriend Julia can't let go of, and the reason she keeps returning to the nightmare. Pedro Merlo takes on the role, and it's a demanding one: a character who is simultaneously a victim, a memory, and possibly something else entirely.
